Civil engineering student at Universitas Jambi, Indonesia, mainly interested in geotechnical engineering. This site has calculators with full working, tools for soil mechanics lab data, practice problems, and my study notes.

About me GitHub

Tools

The tools themselves are in Indonesian. Numbers use a comma as the decimal separator.

Axial capacity of bored piles from SPT

Reese & Wright (1977) method with a soil profile and step-by-step calculation.

Bearing capacity of shallow foundations

General bearing capacity equation with shape and depth factors and a groundwater correction.

Consolidation settlement

Normally and over-consolidated clay, e–log σ' diagram, and settlement over time.

Soil mechanics lab data tools

Sixteen tools for soil mechanics lab data, from water content to consolidation, CPT, and SPT, with export to Word and Excel.

Geotechnical practice problems

Nine problem types with random numbers, automatic checking, and worked solutions.

Why the results can be checked

Every result shows the given data, the formula with its source, the substituted numbers, and the assumptions, so it can be compared with a hand calculation. Each change is tested automatically against a separate Python calculation. The source status of each tool (original, secondary, or not yet verified) is listed in the README.

A study aid. These tools do not replace design checks by a geotechnical engineer.
