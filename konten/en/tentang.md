## How I use AI in my studies

I use Claude in three roles, depending on the task:

| Role | Used for | What I ask for |
|---|---|---|
| Tutor | Learning new material | Theory first, then an example, then an exercise I solve myself |
| Discussion partner | Choosing a thesis title, arguments, methods | Several options with their pros and cons; I make the decision |
| Technical assistant | Calculations, checking files, preparing documents | Real calculations that are checked twice, with assumptions and sources written out |

What I do not hand over to AI: final decisions, field data, and the last check before anything is submitted. The details are on the [Limits and ethics](../cara-memakai-ai/batasan-dan-etika.md) page (in Indonesian).
